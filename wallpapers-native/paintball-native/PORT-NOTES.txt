Original-derived native Paintball reproduction. Original PNG textures and decoded Niagara motion/color/alpha curves, 9 deterministic sampled variants. Concentration controls pigment absorption independently of erosion, alpha and geometry; brightness controls visible pigment lightness; size controls geometry. Editable HLSL: native/windows-player/materials/paintball. Update material chunks with tools/compile-paintball-materials.py. Windows D3D11 WARP tests verify independent controls across all 9 variants. Color transfer assumes sRGB input and uses an explicit display transform; not a pixel-identical original Unreal runtime. Original camera shake/hold-reset policies remain separate.

Center size scales the opening ripple. The pigment hold and fade settings independently control the time before the settled paint fades and the length of its transition. The fade defaults to 800 ms. The shader uses local effect time and the selected spray speed. The controls use the existing named material uniforms and are covered by the per-variant render test.

After updating the material sources, build the stage_atlas_paintball-native CMake target in Release. It compiles all thirteen DX11 material binaries (.efkmatd) from their matching .efkmat files. The canvas still renders once on the selected adapter at 1920 x 720.
