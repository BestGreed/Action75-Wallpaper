Night Orbit Trio D3D12 author package

The original Action75 Wallpaper FireworksScene CPU seed generation, trajectory
data, variant rules, layer grading and Fireworks HLSL are retained under the
project Apache-2.0 license (LICENSE). They were copied into this self-contained
author project from native/windows-player/src/FireworksScene.cpp and its related
headers/materials before the D3D12 adaptation. These source paths document
provenance only; the build does not include or link the player's private source.

src/PatternData.inc: original deterministic seed generation.
src/Simulation.inc: original configuration, lifespan, selection and burst state.
src/Fireworks.hlsl and LayerColor.hlsli: original rendering equations.
tests/reference: frozen original D3D11 reference, test executable only.

nlohmann/json is distributed under its MIT license in vendor/nlohmann-LICENSE.txt.
The public Action75 ABI headers are copied under sdk/.
